# Pact Fitness — Privacy Policy (DRAFT for lawyer review)

## The short version
- We collect what you give us to build your plan, log workouts and run pacts.
- Health check answers, pain notes, body stats and age are **visible only to you**.
- Your pact partner sees your name, your workouts and whether you trained at your gym. Nothing else.
- We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.
- You can delete your account and all your data from the Me tab at any time.

## What we collect
| What | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Sign-in | You (and us) |
| Name you choose, optional Venmo username | Shown to your pact partner | You and your partner |
| Optional birth year, height, weight, sex | Personalize suggestions | Only you |
| Health check answers | Turn on gentle mode if needed | Only you |
| "Something hurts" areas | Pause weight suggestions for that area | Only you |
| Goals, schedule, equipment, HIIT preference, diet plans | Build your plan | Only you |
| Workouts, sets, weights, reps, effort ratings | Coach suggestions, history | You and your partner |
| Pact goal, stake amount, weekly results, IOUs, approval and pause requests | Run the pact | You and your partner. Pause reasons are never collected. |
| "Verified at gym" yes/no | Badge on workouts | You and your partner |
| Push notification token and your notification settings | Send the notifications you turn on | Only you |

**Location.** When you save your gym, its location is kept only on your phone. When you start a workout, the app checks your location once on your phone to answer "at the gym: yes or no". Only that yes/no is sent to our servers. We never store where you are.

## How we use it
To run the app: build plans, show coach suggestions, count pact workouts, settle weeks, send the notifications you choose, and keep your account secure. We may use aggregated, de-identified statistics to improve the app.

## Who we share it with
- **Your pact partner**, as described above.
- **Service providers** that run the app for us: Supabase (database and sign-in, hosted in the United States), Expo (push notification delivery), Apple (App Store and notification delivery), and [email provider] (sign-in emails). They may only use your data to provide their service to us.
- **The law**: if required by valid legal process.

We don't sell or "share" personal information for advertising, and we don't use health information for advertising.

## How long we keep it
While your account is open. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our database right away and from backups within [30] days. Your partner's own records of past pact weeks may still show your name and the result.

## Your choices and rights
- See and edit most of your information in the app; delete your account from the Me tab.
- Turn notifications and gentle mode on or off in the Me tab.
- California residents can ask to know, correct or delete their personal information, and to limit use of sensitive personal information. We use it only to provide the app. Email [privacy email]. We won't discriminate against you for using these rights.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules make sure each person can only read their own private information and their partner's shared workouts. No system is perfectly secure.

## Children
The app is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

We'll post changes here and tell you in the app if they're significant.
